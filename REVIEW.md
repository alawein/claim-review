# Review priorities

Report concrete changed-line defects first, with a short explanation and the
smallest relevant evidence. If none are found, say so and identify material
untested behavior. Do not invent executed tests or interpret self-reported
reviewer labels as authenticated people.

Check exact source hashes, stale-history preservation, UTF-16/code-point selector
boundaries, v0.2/v0.3 packet compatibility and rejection without replacing valid
work. The offline product must remain one HTML file without network/model calls
or default storage access. Development-only consumers must stay out of the bundle.

For release changes, check one canonical build, identical attested/distributed
bytes, retained artifacts on publication failure, narrow job permissions and
retries that verify existing versions/assets instead of replacing them.
Synthetic study fixtures are not empirical evidence. Independent collection,
original AIS labels, supplied ALCE scores and adjudication must remain distinct.
The entire Dependabot family is excluded from this authorized scope.
